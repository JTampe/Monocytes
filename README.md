# Human Monocytes Studies

R analysis scripts for human monocyte subtype expression of neuro-inflammation and –regeneration-related genes linked to age, sex, stroke and post-stroke recovery.

Tampé, J. F., …, Kokaia, Z. (2024), PLOS ONE,  
“The expression of human monocyte subtype genes related to neuroinflammation and regeneration is linked to age and sex.”  
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0300946
  
Tampé, J. F., …, Zaal Kokaia (2026), Research Square,  
“Time-dependent changes in monocyte subsets and gene expression patterns are associated with long-term recovery in patients with ischemic stroke”  
https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8980124/v1
